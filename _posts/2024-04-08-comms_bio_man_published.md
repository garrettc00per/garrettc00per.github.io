Our study, XPO1 inhibition as a therapeutic strategy for favorable histology Wilms tumor, in part driven by TRIP13 suppression is published in Communications Biology.

The work establishes patient-derived Wilms tumor cell lines as faithful preclinical models, identifies XPO1 as a therapeutic vulnerability through loss-of-function screening, and demonstrates that combining the FDA-approved XPO1 inhibitor KPT-330 with doxorubicin produces durable remissions in vivo — findings that have contributed to an active Phase II clinical trial (NCT05985161).

The results can be found [online](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06140-6).
